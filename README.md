# 🔋 Battery Management System (BMS) for Electric Vehicles

> An intelligent Battery Management System (BMS) designed for Electric Vehicles (EVs) that enhances battery safety, efficiency, and reliability through automatic temperature control, dual-battery management, real-time voltage monitoring, and intelligent battery switching.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

The **Battery Management System (BMS)** is an EV-based smart power management solution developed to improve battery performance and ensure uninterrupted vehicle operation.

The system utilizes **two rechargeable batteries**, where one battery powers the vehicle while the other is charged using the electrical energy generated by **four DC motors** during vehicle operation. Once the charging battery reaches its predefined voltage level, the system automatically switches the batteries, allowing continuous power delivery and efficient battery utilization.

To enhance battery safety, the system continuously monitors battery temperature. When the temperature exceeds the safe operating limit, a cooling fan is automatically activated to reduce heat and protect the battery from overheating.

In addition, the system provides real-time monitoring of battery voltages, charging voltage, battery status, and cooling fan operation.

# ⚙️ Working Principle

### Step 1 – Vehicle Startup

- Battery 1 powers the entire electric vehicle.
- Battery 2 remains connected to the charging circuit.

### Step 2 – Power Generation

- Four DC motors drive the vehicle.
- During vehicle operation, the generated electrical power is directed to the charging circuit for charging the secondary battery.

### Step 3 – Temperature Monitoring

- The temperature sensor continuously monitors the battery temperature.
- If the temperature exceeds the predefined limit, the cooling fan is automatically activated.
- The fan turns OFF once the temperature returns to the normal operating range.

### Step 4 – Voltage Monitoring

The Arduino continuously measures:

- Battery 1 Voltage
- Battery 2 Voltage
- Charging Voltage
- Active Battery Status
- Charging Battery Status

### Step 5 – Automatic Battery Switching

- Battery 1 powers the system while Battery 2 charges.
- Once Battery 2 reaches full charge, the Arduino automatically switches the power supply.
- Battery 2 begins powering the vehicle.
- Battery 1 is connected to the charging circuit.
- This process repeats continuously, ensuring uninterrupted vehicle operation.

|---------------|------------------|
