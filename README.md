# 🔋 Battery Management System (BMS) for Electric Vehicles

## 📖 Overview

The **Battery Management System (BMS)** is an innovative Electric Vehicle (EV)-based project that improves battery efficiency, safety, and vehicle reliability through intelligent battery monitoring and automatic power management.

This project uses **two rechargeable batteries**, where one battery powers the electric vehicle while the other battery is charged using the electrical energy generated by **four DC motors** during vehicle operation. Once the charging battery reaches its full charge, the system automatically switches the batteries, ensuring uninterrupted power and continuous charging.

The system also monitors battery temperature and automatically activates a cooling fan whenever the temperature exceeds the safe operating limit.

# ⚙️ Working Principle

1. The vehicle starts using **Battery 1**.
2. **Four DC motors** drive the vehicle.
3. While rotating, the DC motors generate electrical energy that is used to charge **Battery 2**.
4. The Arduino continuously monitors:
   - Battery temperature
   - Battery 1 voltage
   - Battery 2 voltage
   - Charging voltage produced by the motors
5. If the battery temperature exceeds the predefined limit, the cooling fan turns ON automatically.
6. When Battery 2 becomes fully charged, the Arduino automatically switches the power supply:
   - Battery 2 powers the vehicle.
   - Battery 1 begins charging.
7. This charging and switching process repeats continuously, providing efficient battery utilization and uninterrupted vehicle operation.

---

# 🔋 Dual Battery Management

### Battery 1 Active

- Battery 1 powers the EV.
- Battery 2 charges from the power generated by the DC motors.

### Battery 2 Fully Charged

- Arduino automatically switches to Battery 2.
- Battery 1 starts charging.

### Battery 2 Active

- Battery 2 powers the EV.
- Battery 1 charges using the generated electrical energy.

---

# 🌡️ Temperature Control

- Temperature sensor continuously measures battery temperature.
- If temperature exceeds the safe limit:
  - Cooling fan automatically turns ON.
- When the temperature returns to normal:
  - Cooling fan automatically turns OFF.

This protects the battery pack from overheating and improves battery life.

---

# ⚡ Power Generation

The project uses **four DC motors** connected to the vehicle.

During vehicle movement:

- The DC motors rotate.
- Mechanical energy is converted into electrical energy.
- The generated electrical power is supplied to the charging circuit.
- The charging circuit safely charges the inactive battery.
- Arduino continuously measures the charging voltage produced.
